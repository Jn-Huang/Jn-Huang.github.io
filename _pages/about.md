Hi! I am Steven (Jin) Huang, a third-year Ph.D. student at the University of Michigan in the [Foreseer Group](https://umich-foreseer.github.io), advised by Prof. [Qiaozhu Mei](http://www-personal.umich.edu/~qmei/). My research interests include Foundation Models for Social Simulation ([BehaviorBench and BeFM1.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24162), [BeFM1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23058)), Mid/Post-training for scientific discovery ([SciLitLLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15545)), Auto-research Agent ([Idea2Plan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24891)).

Previously, I completed bachelor's degree at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor. I've also had the privilege of working with Prof. [Jiaqi Ma](https://www.jiaqima.com) and Prof. [Danai Koutra](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~dkoutra/). I interned at Microsoft Research in Redmond, DP Technology and Intel.
